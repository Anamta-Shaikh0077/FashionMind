# FashionMind — Project Requirements

## 1. Project Overview

**Project Name:** FashionMind

**Project Type:** Retail, Fashion and Supply Chain Intelligence Platform

**Objective:** Build a data-driven platform that helps businesses analyse sales, profitability, inventory and customer behaviour to make better business decisions.

## 2. Business Problem

Retail and fashion businesses often struggle with:

- Overstocking and stock shortages.
- Unprofitable products and excessive discounting.
- Uncertainty about customer purchasing behaviour.
- Difficulty identifying seasonal sales patterns.
- Poor demand and inventory planning.

FashionMind aims to turn business data into useful insights and recommendations.

## 3. Target Users

- Retail business managers.
- Inventory planners.
- Business analysts.
- Fashion retailers.
- Supply chain and operations teams.

## 4. Project Modules

1. Sales Intelligence — analyse revenue, sales trends and product performance.
2. Profitability Intelligence — identify profitable and underperforming products.
3. Inventory Intelligence — identify slow-moving stock and potential stock shortages.
4. Customer Intelligence — analyse purchasing behaviour and customer segments.
5. Demand Intelligence — explore and forecast future product demand.
6. Insight Engine — present evidence-based business recommendations.

## 5. Technology Stack

- Python
- SQL and SQLite
- Excel
- Power BI
- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript
- Flask
- Git and GitHub

Pandas and predictive modelling will be incorporated when the environment supports them.

## 6. Initial Dataset

UCI Online Retail II.

The dataset will be used for initial transaction analysis. Its UK business context and historical dates will be clearly documented.

## 7. Expected First Release

- Cleaned transaction dataset.
- SQL database.
- Sales analysis.
- Profitability analysis.
- Customer analysis.
- Power BI dashboard.
- Documented business insights.

## 8. Data Integrity

All findings must be supported by the available data.

Missing values, assumptions, limitations and data transformations must be documented. Synthetic data must never be presented as real customer or business data.
